About TrackDeals EU

How the deals are found, how we make money, and what to be aware of.

How deals are found

We scan public EU marketplaces (Autodoc and eBay, German marketplace) for common car parts. A product is shown as a "deal" only when its price is at least 15% below the average price of the same part in the same search batch, so "discounts" are measured against the real market, not a pumped-up RRP.

Affiliate disclosure

  • Links marked with "↗" on this site are affiliate links (eBay Partner Network). If you follow one and buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Autodoc product links are regular product links unless a partner programme is configured.
  • TrackDeals EU is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Autodoc, eBay, or any car or parts manufacturer.
  • Prices and availability come from the source sites and can change at any time; the "seen" date shows when a price was last confirmed.

Data sourcing note

The site indexes parts from public listings and is not a shop. Always verify fitment (make, model, year, engine) with the seller before buying. We try to show which car makes each part mentions, but the final compatibility check is your responsibility together with the seller.
